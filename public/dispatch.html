MSCI

MSCI freezes Turkey index changes; downgrade risk on the table

On October 15, MSCI froze upward changes to its Turkey equity indexes: no new additions and no increases to the foreign inclusion factor. The reasons: concerns about coordinated trading in small-cap stocks and unreliable free-float pricing. MSCI will reassess in its 2027 review; it warned that if reforms stall by June 2027, it may open a consultation on downgrading Turkey to ‘frontier market’ status.
