Don’t have a CSV yet? Here is where to find it

Not from the statements page — that gives PDFs. Sign in on a desktop browser, click into the account itself, and look for a download link above the list of transactions. Pick Excel or CSV and the months you want.

At Bank of America it is a small download icon just above the transactions, once you are inside an account. If that link lands you on a sign-in page, sign in and click it again.

Download generously: re-importing a month changes nothing, so overlapping ranges are safe. Load every account — money moved between them is recognised as an internal transfer, not spending.

Worth knowing before you interpret

Cash counts as spending when you take it out, not when you spend it. A statement cannot see the second, but you can:

  • give a withdrawal a category of its own, or split it into the things you actually bought — see your withdrawals.

Whatever you leave stays as Cash spending, which means money gone and unaccounted for.

A deposit is not a withdrawal coming back. Money paid in is new money arriving, and the statement cannot say where from — so the two never cancel. $300 taken out and $1,200 paid in is $300 spent and $1,200 arrived, not $900 back.

  • if it really was cash you took out and did not spend, say so on that one deposit and it comes off your cash spending — see your deposits.
