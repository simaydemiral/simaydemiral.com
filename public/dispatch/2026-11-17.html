Southwest hydropower fell sharply through the summer

The US Southwest generated 28% less hydroelectricity from April through August than its 10-year average, according to Energy Information Administration data, as a prolonged Colorado River Basin drought pushed Lake Powell and Lake Mead near the minimum levels their turbines require. The EIA projects national hydropower output will hit its lowest level in over a decade in 2026 and dip further in 2027; October and early November rains lifted Lake Powell more than 20 feet from its mid-September low.
