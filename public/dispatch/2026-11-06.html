Parliament ratifies three-way defense pact with Saudi Arabia, Pakistan

Turkish lawmakers approved the Mecca Joint Defense Agreement, under which an external armed attack on Turkey, Saudi Arabia or Pakistan would be treated as an attack on all three, with assistance that can include military support determined through consultations. Opposition lawmakers who voted against it argued Turkey could be pulled into Saudi Arabia's confrontation with the Houthis or India-Pakistan tensions; New Party leader Özgür Özel said his party would never support Turkish soldiers being sent to Yemen.
