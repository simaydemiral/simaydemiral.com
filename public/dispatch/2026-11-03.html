Ai

Economists pitch incentive design for AI alignment

Alignment researchers are turning to reverse game theory, or mechanism design, which builds rules to elicit desired behavior rather than trying to interpret a model's internals — on the premise that we may never understand frontier models well enough to verify alignment directly. A recent preprint by MIT economist Stephen Morris with colleagues at Yale and Columbia proposes rewarding a model for predicting a second model's answers, or pairing oppositely biased agents, to make deliberate underperformance on safety tests a losing strategy.

KEY   1 preprint paper · authors from MIT, Yale and Columbia · second approach proposed by Princeton's Elad Hazan
