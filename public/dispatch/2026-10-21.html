Recursive releases laptop coding model built largely by AI

Big Three banks take most of equities trading boom

The five largest investment banks have generated about $67 billion in equities revenue through Sept. 30, roughly $7 billion more than in all of 2025, according to company filings, with Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan and Morgan Stanley accounting for nearly 80% of the total. Bank executives and Wells Fargo analyst Mike Mayo attribute the lead to prime brokerage lending to hedge funds levering up on AI hardware, hyperscalers and chip suppliers, plus balance sheets big enough to absorb single-trade risks smaller rivals must decline.
