Higher education board plans three-year undergraduate degrees

Council of Higher Education President Erol Özvar said Saturday that Turkey is preparing to cut most undergraduate degrees from four years to three, a model of 180 credits drawing on European examples, citing falling fertility, delayed marriage and the time it takes young people to enter working life. Study finds İğneada floodplain forest shrinking under drought

Researchers from Bartın and İstanbul universities, publishing in the journal Forests, found that the Erikli floodplain forest shrank by 16 percent between 1984 and 2014 while wetlands in Sakagöl disappeared or deteriorated, attributing the change to drought, water stress and growing human pressure. They reported that regional average temperatures rose 1.8°C over 44 years, said key species such as ash and pedunculate oak are declining, and warned that reduced water could cut how much carbon Europe's largest floodplain forest stores.

KEY   16% shrinkage, or 109 hectares, 1984-2014 · +1.8°C over 44 years · semi-arid climate projected across 89% of the region by the end of the century if warming worsens
