Covid lockdowns lowered global fire activity, study finds

A team led by Jianghao Wang of the Chinese Academy of Sciences used the early-2020 lockdowns as a natural experiment, since human mobility dropped sharply at different times across countries while weather did not shift in the same way. Their study in Cell Reports Sustainability reports that the lower human activity had the net effect of reducing average fire activity, drawing on regional lockdown orders, mobile phone location data and NASA and NOAA Suomi NPP satellite records.
