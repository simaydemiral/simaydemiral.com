DeepMind formally verifies parts of Signal protocol

Google DeepMind researchers used agentic AI to mathematically verify several of the Signal protocol's cryptographic guarantees, using a framework called AlphaProof Code. The work did not cover the full Signal mobile app, and researchers say scaling the approach broadly remains a long way off, though on older Signal versions the tool correctly flagged known, long-fixed vulnerabilities.

Plurai raises seed round for AI agent simulations

Plurai, founded by two former Nexar employees, raised seed funding to build a simulation layer that stress-tests AI agents against edge cases using synthetic digital twins of a company's systems — borrowing the approach used to validate self-driving software. Co-founder Ilan Kadar says the gathered data can also post-train smaller open-weight models, which tests with Nvidia and Nebius reportedly showed outperforming mid-tier frontier models at lower cost.
